King Intellectual Asset Management is a boutique IP firm on Colorado's Front Range. We represent companies from garage startups to Fortune 500 technology leaders, in patents and trademarks from strategy through enforcement.

What we do

Patent prosecution and portfolio strategy

Applications built to be enforced. Claims come first, the specification teaches the invention, and each drafting choice accounts for how infringement would actually be proven. In prosecution we work with examiners toward claims that are commercially meaningful and genuinely valid.

Opinions, post-grant and litigation

Validity, infringement and freedom-to-operate opinions; inter partes review and other PTAB proceedings; and patent and trademark litigation at the trial and appellate levels. Litigating patents is what taught us how to write them.

Trademarks

Clearance, registration and prosecution, TTAB proceedings, and portfolio management, for startups and established consumer brands alike.

Chad King

Founder and principal attorney

Chad has spent more than twenty years as primary outside IP counsel to companies ranging from garage startups to Fortune 500 corporations, as well as government entities. He has written and litigated patents in roughly equal measure. That experience shapes both how he attacks patents in litigation and how he protects them when drafting.

He believes an IP strategy has to follow the client's business strategy, and that the aim is to optimize the value of a portfolio rather than merely its size.

An engineer by training, Chad worked in IT at IBM before law school and clerked for Judge Jerry E. Smith of the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit after it.

Away from the office he is with his wife Melissa, their adult children and two Bernadoodles, on a golf course he loves and hates in equal measure, or deep in a sci-fi novel.
